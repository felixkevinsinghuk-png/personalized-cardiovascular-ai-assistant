Research Tool Only

This application is an academic research prototype. It uses AI models trained on retinal fundus images to estimate cardiovascular risk indicators. Outputs must not be used for clinical decision-making, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified medical professional for health concerns.

How It Works
  1. Preprocessing
    Black border cropped · Resized to 224×224 · ImageNet normalised
  2. DR Grading (ResNet-50)
    Classifies diabetic retinopathy severity — 5 grades (0–4)
  3. CVD Risk Scoring (EfficientNet-B4)
    Detects hypertensive retinopathy markers — outputs 0.0–1.0 risk score
  4. Weighted Fusion
    Combines both scores (w=0.4/0.6) into a single fused risk score
  5. Grad-CAM Explanation
    Highlights retinal regions that influenced the prediction
  6. AI Report (BioMistral / MedGemma)
    Local medical LLM generates a structured written explanation
